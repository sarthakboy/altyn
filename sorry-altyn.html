My Dearest Altyn,

I spoke too much. I filled the air between us with words that had no right to exist — careless, sharp, and at times cruel. You, who only ever deserved to feel adored and safe, felt something else entirely because of me. I am so, so deeply sorry.

Disrespect is one of the worst things one person can show another — and I showed it to the very person I treasure most. That haunts me. Not out of guilt alone, but because I know your worth, and my words contradicted everything I feel for you.

From today, I choose silence before a harmful word. I vow to listen twice as much as I speak. I will treat your voice, your feelings, your dignity as the sacred, precious things they are.

You deserve a love that lifts you up — never one that makes you feel small. You are everything to me, and I will spend every day earning back the trust my words once cost.

Yours, with a humbled heart 💙
